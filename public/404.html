Why cats?

Because every cat I've loved came from somewhere a little lost.

When my brother and I were small, my mom took in a tiny stray in a village in Italy. We called her Fleckchen, German for "Spotty", because she had spots all over. She came back to Germany with us in an old Volkswagen, together with her one surviving kitten, a little boy we called Mini. He grew into the toughest and sweetest cat on the street.

Years later, when I had kids of my own, we adopted two cats from a shelter in Honolulu, Nicholas and Christopher. Best cats in the world. So if a broken link can help one more cat find a home, that's a good use for a broken link.
